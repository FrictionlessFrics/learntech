What we record

We record what a regulator did: an improvement notice, a licence suspension, a seizure, a penalty, a prosecution. We do not repeat what an inspector says they saw. Each entry gives the date, the authority, the legal section where the source states one, and a link to the government document it came from.

Notices get complied with, withdrawn, or overturned on appeal. Every entry has a status and the date we last confirmed it. Withdrawn and overturned actions stay visible but are struck through, so the record stays accurate.

How records are matched to a business

Every business is identified by its 14-digit FSSAI licence number, never by name alone. Government documents, licence records and maps all spell names differently, and matching on names puts actions on the wrong business.

An action is linked to a licence only if the source document gives the licence number, or a reviewer has confirmed both the name and the address against the licence record on FoSCoS. Each entry says which.

Why "no actions found" is not a clean record

Most food-safety enforcement in India is never published online. Improvement notices under Section 32 and adjudication orders under Section 68 can only be obtained through Right to Information requests. Press notes cover the actions an authority chose to announce, which leans towards large brands and busy areas.

So when we show "No actions found in the sources we checked", it means exactly that. Each entry lists the sources we checked.

Where the data comes from

  • FSSAI hygiene rating directory (the older portal at hygiene.fssai.gov.in). We do not collect from pages whose robots.txt forbids it.
  • FoSCoS licence register: licence status and validity, checked by licence number only.
  • Maharashtra FDA press notes, read by a person. We record the facts, not the article.
  • Court and consumer commission orders, FSSAI press releases and RTI replies, entered by hand.

Our collectors identify themselves, go slowly, and stop when a site asks for a CAPTCHA.

Corrections

If an entry is wrong, or an order has been withdrawn or overturned, please ask for a correction and include a link to or copy of the document. Corrections are recorded publicly in the project's history.
